What is this?

Make Stonk Great Again is a community-driven movement. A lot of people in the trenches are tired of how things feel on Pump.fun right now, and we think the culture deserves a fresh start somewhere else.

So we're pointing attention, creators, communities and volume toward the Stonk launchpad.

  • The trenches deserve better.
  • Communities deserve better.
  • Real builders deserve more attention.
  • Memecoin culture needs a fresh start.
  • We want to help make Stonk the next home for the trenches.

Independent community project. Not built, run or endorsed by Stonk. Nothing here is financial advice.

The Trenches Need Change — Properties

THE TRENCHES NEED CHANGE.

These are the concerns people in the community keep raising about the current launchpad culture:

  • Rug pulls and scam projects
  • Too much short-term promotion
  • Influencer-driven hype cycles
  • Low-quality launches
  • Fading confidence in the current launchpad culture

These are opinions shared across the community. They are not claims about any specific person or project.

COMPETITION IS HEALTHY.

We think the memecoin community does better when launchpads have to compete for it. One place shouldn't own the whole culture.

  • Explore Stonk and launch there
  • Back creators who actually build
  • Build healthier communities around coins

Do your own research on every coin, on every launchpad.

THE CULTURE DOESN'T BELONG TO ONE LAUNCHPAD.

IT BELONGS TO THE PEOPLE.
